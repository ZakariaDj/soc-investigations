# SOC Investigations

A collection of my practical cybersecurity investigations and Blue Team exercises.

This repository documents my progression toward a career in SOC Analysis, with a focus on investigating security incidents, analyzing evidence, identifying Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), understanding attacker behavior, and documenting findings.

## Areas of Investigation

* Network traffic analysis
* Log analysis
* Email and phishing analysis
* Authentication and session analysis
* Incident investigation
* Timeline reconstruction
* Indicator of Compromise (IOC) identification
* MITRE ATT&CK mapping
* Evidence-based reporting

## Investigation Process

Each investigation follows a structured approach:

1. Understand the incident and available evidence
2. Collect and analyze relevant artifacts
3. Identify suspicious activity
4. Correlate findings and reconstruct the timeline
5. Identify relevant IOCs
6. Map observed activity to appropriate techniques
7. Document findings and conclusions

## Purpose

This repository is part of my SOC Analyst journey and serves as a record of my practical experience and progression.

The goal is to document not only the final findings, but also the investigation process, evidence, reasoning, and conclusions behind each investigation.

More investigations and reports will be added as I continue developing my practical SOC and Blue Team skills.
